Privacy Policy

Jellyfish is a watch face for Wear OS watches, published by Hamza Bharmal.

What we collect: nothing. The watch face contains no app code. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it has no ads, analytics or account.

What it uses on your watch: the time, and your watch's motion sensor for a small parallax effect. This happens entirely on the watch. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Children: Jellyfish is not directed at children under 13.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
